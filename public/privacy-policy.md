# MedRoster Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

MedRoster is published by **Blaze Studios** (`com.blazestudios.medroster`).

MedRoster ("we", "our", or "the app") is a hospital workforce rostering application for Ghana Health Service facilities.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** Email address and display name when you register or sign in via Firebase Authentication.
- **Hospital data:** Hospital name, departments, staff records, and schedules that you or your organization enter into the app.
- **Usage data:** Standard app diagnostics and error logs to keep the service reliable.

## How we use information

- To authenticate you and provide access to your hospital's roster data.
- To store and display schedules, staff directories, and related workforce management features.
- To maintain and improve app security and performance.

## Data storage and security

- Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Auth.
- Application data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and accessed through our secured API.
- Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with service providers required to operate the app (Firebase/Google Cloud) and as required by law.
